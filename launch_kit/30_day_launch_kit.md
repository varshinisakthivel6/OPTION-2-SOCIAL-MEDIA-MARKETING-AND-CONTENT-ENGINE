# 30-day launch kit — draft
Owners: growth/content owner (calendar, copy, analytics); product owner (feature claims, landing page); designer/editor (final video/visuals); reviewer (rights, accessibility, accuracy). Confirm named owners.
Week 1: verify product observations and claims; audit IncuBrix plus five peers; finalise assets; publish only after approvals.
Week 2: maintain cadence; review reach, engagement, saves, shares, clicks and sign-ups.
Week 3: test alternate hooks/CTAs; adapt winning topics; inspect landing-page friction.
Week 4: compare against baseline; document costs, learnings and next experiments.
Cadence hypothesis: Instagram 4/week; YouTube Shorts 3/week; LinkedIn 3/week.
Budget: organic-first; optional paid boost $0 until baseline and tracking are verified; any spend needs written approval.
Targets: establish baseline first; report reach, engagement rate (state formula), UTM clicks, landing conversion and sign-ups. Never present guessed targets as achieved.
Risks: unverified product claims (product-owner approval); copyright/consent (owned/licensed assets); AI errors (human review); tracking failures (test UTMs); platform policy changes (recheck rules); privacy (no private scraping).
Daily workflow: review source and calendar; generate/adapt; fact/rights/accessibility review; add UTM; schedule; record URL and metrics; log next experiment.
