# Social strategy — working hypothesis
Audience: independent podcasters, YouTubers, expert-led creators; secondary audience: freelance editors and small content teams in the listed English-speaking markets.
Platforms: Instagram (Reels/carousels), YouTube Shorts (short educational video), LinkedIn (professional creator operators and teams).
Pillars: creator workflow; repurposing; audience growth; content strategy; production efficiency; creator education.
Voice: helpful, direct, specific, creator-first; one idea per post; no hype, guaranteed outcomes or unsupported metrics; never claim a product feature until verified.
Initial cadence hypothesis: Instagram 4/week; YouTube Shorts 3/week; LinkedIn 3/week. Adjust only after observing real analytics.
Conversion path: useful post → relevant landing page → product explanation/demo → sign-up. Use UTMs and measure clicks and completed sign-ups.
Validate the audience, product positioning and claims with the live product and real account analytics before publishing.
