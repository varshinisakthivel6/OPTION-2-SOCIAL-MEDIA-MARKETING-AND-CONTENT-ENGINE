# IB-28 — Avoid the three-topic short video

Platform: YouTube Shorts
Format: Short video
Pillar: Content strategy

## Hook
Avoid the three-topic short video

## Caption / script
If a 45-second video tries to explain three unrelated ideas, viewers may remember none. Choose one promise, support it with one example, and end with one action.

## CTA
Try one idea per Short.
